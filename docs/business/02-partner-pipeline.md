# Partner Pipeline

| Segment | Target Type | Value For Partner | BiblioGABON Ask | First Outreach Asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Universities | Public and private higher education institutions | Digital library and usage reporting | Pilot agreement, content access, student communication | Institutional one-pager |
| Teachers | Faculty and researchers | Controlled publication and visibility | Voluntary content deposits and ambassador role | Teacher contributor brief |
| Telecoms | Mobile operators | Education impact and data/payment usage | Billing or bundle partnership | Usage traction report |
| Sponsors | Banks, foundations, RSE programs | Measurable education impact | Funded access cohort | Sponsored access proposal |
| Public institutions | Ministries and agencies | National academic infrastructure | Endorsement, coordination, possible funding | Policy alignment brief |

## Partner Sequence

Start with partners that improve product proof before partners that require negotiation leverage.

1. Teacher ambassadors: secure useful content and credibility.
2. Universities and schools: validate institutional need and access model.
3. Sponsors: fund access cohorts and create visible impact.
4. Payment aggregators: make B2C and institutional top-ups operational.
5. Telecoms: negotiate bundles or billing once active usage data exists.
6. Public institutions: seek alignment, endorsement, or coordination once the platform can demonstrate traction.

## First Outreach Assets

- Institutional one-pager: problem, platform value, pilot proposal, reporting promise.
- Teacher contributor brief: rights, publication workflow, visibility, withdrawal rules.
- Sponsored access proposal: target cohort, cost model, reporting, impact metrics.
- Usage traction report: active readers, reading sessions, searches, domains, retention, testimonials.
- Policy alignment brief: national academic access, digital transformation, data protection, sovereignty.

## Pipeline Fields To Track

- Partner name.
- Segment.
- Contact person.
- Stage: identified, contacted, meeting scheduled, proposal sent, negotiation, signed, lost.
- Strategic value.
- Commercial value.
- Content value.
- Required internal owner.
- Next action and date.
- Risks or blockers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each partner segment has a clear value exchange.
- Outreach starts with credibility and content before complex telecom negotiations.
- Sponsored access is treated as a repeatable offer, not a one-off donation.
- Public-sector engagement supports legitimacy without making the startup dependent on public ownership.
