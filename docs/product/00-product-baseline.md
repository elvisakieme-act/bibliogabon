# BiblioGABON Product Baseline

## Product Definition

BiblioGABON is a national academic digital library for Gabon, carried by an independent startup. It centralizes, protects, indexes, and distributes academic resources for students, teachers, researchers, institutions, and sponsored access partners.

The product is a launchable platform, not a simple showcase site. It must support public discovery, restricted reading, institutional access, individual subscriptions, sponsored access, document ingestion, rights governance, administration, and usage reporting.

## Strategic Positioning

BiblioGABON is positioned as academic infrastructure for Gabon: more focused than a general reading platform, more scalable than an internal school repository, and more secure than informal file sharing. Its value comes from the combination of national academic catalog, protected distribution, deep search, mobile-first access, and clear document governance.

## Users And Organizations

Core users are visitors, students, teachers/authors, institution administrators, BiblioGABON content administrators, BiblioGABON super administrators, and sponsor partners.

Organizations represent universities, schools, enterprises, sponsors, foundations, or public institutions that finance or manage access for a population. Users may hold individual access, organization access, sponsored access, or a combination of these.

## Business Model

The business model is hybrid:

- B2B institutional access is the primary revenue engine.
- B2C micro-subscription keeps access possible for users outside covered institutions.
- Sponsored access funds impact cohorts and broad national adoption.
- Content revenue sharing may apply to authors, publishers, or institutions when documents are distributed under commercial or rights-based agreements.

## Product Scope

Launch scope includes:

- Public catalog and document metadata.
- User accounts, roles, organizations, memberships, quotas, and entitlements.
- Document upload, private file storage, processing, and publication workflow.
- Secure online reader with access checks and no raw file exposure.
- Search across metadata and document text.
- Billing foundations for institutions, individual users, and sponsored campaigns.
- Back-office administration for documents, rights, users, organizations, and support.
- Usage reporting for product, business, and institutional stakeholders.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- The product is not derived from the current maquette; the maquette is reserved for later UI/UX inspiration.
- BiblioGABON is designed as a complete launchable platform, not a minimal MVP.
- Raw document files are stored privately and never exposed directly.
- Every document has an owner, publication status, access rule, and withdrawal rule.
- Institutional access and individual access coexist.
- Users attached to one institution may access the national catalog according to their subscription rights.
- Mobile-first access and low-bandwidth behavior are product priorities.

## Out Of Scope For Launch

- A native mobile application before the web mobile experience proves demand.
- Complex AI recommendation systems before reliable search and catalog usage exist.
- Automatic publication without rights review.
- Full university library migration services before the first institutional pilots validate the operating model.
- Telecom billing integration before usage data justifies partnership negotiation.
