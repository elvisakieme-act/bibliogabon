# Content Rights And Publication Governance

## Document Categories

- Voluntary teacher deposit: content submitted by its author for publication.
- Institutional fund: content supplied by a school, university, archive, lab, or organization under contract.
- Student work: thesis, dissertation, report, internship report, or academic project requiring explicit authorization.
- Open resource: public domain or openly licensed content verified before publication.
- Commercial partner content: publisher or author content distributed under revenue-sharing or licensing agreement.

## Ownership Rules

Each document must have a declared owner before publication. The owner may be an individual author, a group of authors, an institution, a publisher, or another rights holder identified by contract.

Ownership records must capture:

- Owner identity and contact.
- Document category.
- Publication authorization.
- Access model: free, subscription, institution-only, sponsored, restricted, or private.
- Withdrawal rule.
- Revenue-sharing rule when applicable.
- Confidentiality constraints.
- Source file retention policy.

Publication ownership is not the same as access sponsorship. An organization can fund access to documents it does not own, and an owner can publish a document that is read by users from many organizations.

## Publication Workflow

Status sequence:

```text
draft -> submitted -> rights_review -> technical_processing -> editorial_review -> published -> withdrawn -> archived
```

Rules:

- A document cannot become published without owner, category, license/access rule, and withdrawal rule.
- Student work requires explicit consent and confidentiality review.
- Technical processing can run before final publication but cannot expose pages publicly.
- Withdrawn documents remain internally traceable and can be republished only through a new validation decision.
- Documents rejected during rights review must keep a rejection reason for internal audit.
- Published documents must expose only approved metadata and approved page assets, never the raw private file.

## Withdrawal Rules

Withdrawal behavior depends on document category:

- Voluntary teacher deposit: the author may request withdrawal, subject to reasonable notice and contract terms.
- Institutional fund: withdrawal follows the institution contract and cannot be unilateral unless defined.
- Student work: withdrawal may be requested by the student, rights holder, or affected institution; confidentiality concerns override commercial goals.
- Open resource: withdrawal happens when license status is invalid, source quality is disputed, or the resource becomes inappropriate.
- Commercial partner content: withdrawal follows the distribution agreement and subscriber notice obligations.

Withdrawal must be reversible internally. The public state changes, but processing records, audit logs, contracts, and metadata history remain available to authorized staff.

## Sensitive Document Policy

Sensitive documents include internship reports, theses, memoires, research data, case studies, administrative documents, and any content containing personal data, company secrets, health data, financial data, or non-public institutional information.

Sensitive document rules:

- Do not publish without explicit written authorization.
- Review for personal data and confidential third-party information.
- Redact or refuse content that cannot be safely distributed.
- Keep a record of consent and reviewer decision.
- Provide a reporting channel for rights holders and affected parties.
- Suspend public access quickly when a credible complaint is received.

## Revenue Sharing Principles

Revenue sharing may apply when a document owner contributes commercial or rights-controlled content. The exact calculation should be contractual, but the product must support these principles:

- Free/open documents can be distributed without revenue sharing when the license allows it.
- Voluntary academic deposits may be unpaid, paid, or impact-based depending on the agreement.
- Commercial partner content can use revenue share, fixed license fee, or hybrid model.
- Usage metrics used for payouts must be auditable.
- Download and offline rights can be priced separately from reading rights.

## Required Contract Templates

- Teacher voluntary publication agreement.
- Institutional archive/fund agreement.
- Student work publication consent.
- Commercial content distribution agreement.
- Sponsored access agreement.
- Organization subscription agreement.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every document category has an ownership and withdrawal model.
- No document can be published without rights review.
- Student work receives special confidentiality treatment.
- Technical processing is separated from public availability.
- Raw files remain private at every workflow state.
- Revenue sharing is treated as a contract-backed rule, not a hard-coded assumption.
